# Privacy Policy for TODAILY

**Last updated: May 2026**

TODAILY is a minimalistic, privacy‑first personal journal and task tracker.
This document explains that the app does **not** collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.

---

## 1. No Personal Data Collection

TODAILY does **not** collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.
The app does not use analytics, tracking tools, advertising SDKs, or remote servers.

Specifically, TODAILY does **not**:

- collect personal information
- track user behavior
- store data on external servers
- share data with third parties
- use cookies or identifiers
- log usage or device information

All your data stays **entirely on your device**.

---

## 2. Local Data Storage Only

All information entered by the user (such as tasks, notes, or schedules) is stored **locally on your device**.
TODAILY has no access to this data and cannot retrieve it.

If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is removed from the device.

---

## 3. Required Permissions

TODAILY uses only the minimum permissions required for core functionality.
The app does **not** require any unnecessary permissions to function.

---

## 4. No Internet Communication

TODAILY does not connect to the internet.
The app operates **entirely offline**. No data is ever sent, received, or loaded from external resources.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

TODAILY does not collect any data from users of any age, including children.
The app is safe to use for all age groups.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If TODAILY’s functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated.

---

## Summary

TODAILY is designed with a simple principle:
